Quantitative Aptitude • Speed, Time & Distance

A train traveling at a speed of 72 km/h crosses a platform of length 280 meters in 26 seconds. How much time will the train take to cross an electric pole?

lightbulb Detailed Step-by-Step Solution
Syllabus Ref: TCS-QA-08
Step 1: Unit Conversion

Convert speed from km/h to m/s by multiplying with factor 5/18:

Speed = 72 × (5 / 18) = 4 × 5 = 20 m/s
Step 2: Total Distance Crossed on Platform

When crossing a platform of length 280 m, the total distance covered equals the sum of the train’s own length (L) and the platform’s length:

Distance = L + 280 = Speed × Time = 20 × 26 = 520 meters
Step 3: Calculating Train Length

Subtract the platform length from total distance to determine train length L:

L = 520 − 280 = 240 meters
Step 4: Crossing the Electric Pole

An electric pole has negligible length. Therefore, distance required is simply the length of the train itself (L):

Time = L / Speed = 240 / 20 = 12 seconds
bolt
Exam Pro Shortcut • Save 45 Seconds

Direct relative deduction formula: Time to cross pole = (Platform crossing time) − (Platform length / Speed)
Time = 26 − (280 / 20) = 26 − 14 = 12 seconds. No need to solve train length separately!
