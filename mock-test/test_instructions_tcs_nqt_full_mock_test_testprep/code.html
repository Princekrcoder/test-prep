Standard CBT Operating Guidelines

1

Server Synchronized Clock

The examination timer is controlled centrally by the TestPrep cluster server. A persistent digital countdown clock will appear in the top-right header throughout the session. When the timer hits 00:00:00, your test will automatically seal and submit without requiring manual confirmation.

2

Question Navigation & Answering Flow

To answer an item, select the circular radio marker adjacent to the desired option. You must click "Save & Next" to store your selection into the secure stream. Clicking directly on a different number on the Question Palette switches questions without recording changes to the active question.

Save & Next Clear Response Mark for Review & Next
3

AI Remote Proctoring & Tab Lockout

This assessment operates under strict enterprise lock-in. Minimizing the browser window, toggling to other applications, splitting the screen, or using shortcuts (Alt+Tab, Cmd+Tab, Esc) triggers an immediate infringement strike. A maximum of 3 strikes are permitted before automatic disqualification.

4

Rough Work & On-Screen Scientific Calculator

An on-screen virtual scientific calculator is provided in the top action strip for Numerical Ability. Physical rough sheets (maximum 2 blank A4 pages) may be kept on your desk; external devices or physical scientific calculators are strictly prohibited.
