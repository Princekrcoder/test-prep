## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a modern, focused, and high-trust EdTech SaaS identity. Built specifically for competitive mock exams, corporate placement tests, and rigorous student assessment workflows, the aesthetic bridges structured enterprise rigor with friendly, motivating academic clarity.

### Personality & Tone
- **Authoritative & Trustworthy:** Mirrors official examination ergonomics to minimize cognitive friction and prepare students for high-stakes test environments.
- **Empowering & Focused:** Reduces sensory overwhelm through calm neutral backgrounds, strict card structures, and intentional focal points.
- **Goal-Oriented:** Leverages vivid functional status accents to mark milestones, performance analytics, answer states, and streak loops.

### Visual Style
The aesthetic aligns with **Modern Corporate / Clean EdTech SaaS**:
- Deep navy primary anchors for deep chrome elements (such as full-height persistent side navigation bars).
- Crisp slate and pure-white layered surface geometry.
- Low-contrast border definitions paired with soft, low-elevation diffusion shadows.
- Vibrant, unmistakable status signaling (exam timer alerts, question palettes, and scorecard metrics).

## Colors

The color architecture is built around high legibility, fast scanability, and rigorous semantic hierarchy. 

### Core Palette Roles
- **Primary (`#1E60F3` / `#2563EB`):** Used for interactive triggers, primary action buttons, active navigation markers, selection rings, and progress indicators.
- **Secondary (`#0F172A` / `#1E293B`):** Deep midnight slate used for dashboard sidebar navigation panels, high-emphasis heading typography, and strong structural anchors.
- **Tertiary & Semantics:**
  - **Success / Answered (`#10B981`):** Indicates answered questions, verified status tags, positive score Deltas, and successful submissions.
  - **Warning / Review (`#F59E0B`):** Applied to flagged items, remaining time warnings, and streak highlights.
  - **Critical / Incorrect (`#EF4444`):** Designates missed questions, negative marks, and error messaging.
  - **Accent Purple (`#8B5CF6`):** Dedicated to category tags, marked-for-review question states, and special badges.

### Surface Tiers
- **Canvas / App Background:** `#F8FAFC` to `#F1F5F9` providing a calm, non-glare foundation.
- **Card Surfaces:** `#FFFFFF` providing maximum text-to-background contrast.
- **Subtle Dividing Borders:** `#E2E8F0` framing content blocks and exam questions without visual clutter.

## Typography

Typography balances structural clarity and effortless extended-session reading.

- **Headings & Quantitative Badges (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Geometric contours add modern punch to section headers, overall score tiles, and timer modules without sacrificing corporate authority.
- **Body & Test Interface (Inter):** Highly legible across varied pixel densities; neutral and un-distracting for dense stem questions, multiple-choice options, and instructional guidelines.
- **Tabular & Examination Palette:** Question numbers and countdown clocks utilize tabular numeric formatting to prevent layout jitter during active timing sessions.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to accommodate two primary operational states: the **Operational Student Dashboard** and the **Full-Screen Exam Testing Console**.

### Layout Models
- **Standard Dashboard:** Fixed 240px–260px dark navigation sidebar paired with an elastic content region pinned to an expansive `1440px` maximum viewport. Inner card gutters maintain a strict 24px (`1.5rem`) rhythm.
- **Active Exam Interface:** Collapses the dark global navigation into an un-distracted, full-width three-pane layout:
  1. *Left Section Strip:* Question categories and subject switcher (approx. 20% width).
  2. *Center Workstation:* Current question stem, high-contrast option inputs, and linear paging controls (approx. 55% width).
  3. *Right Palette Strip:* Numbered question state matrix, legend indicators, and summary metrics (approx. 25% width).

### Responsive Adaptation
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Full 12-column workspace, multi-column dashboard card decks, and pinned examination side-strips.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Side navigation converts to an off-canvas drawer or collapsed icon rail (64px width). Exam palette condenses into a sticky bottom drawer or slide-over sheet.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stack with full-width cards, fixed floating bottom action bars (Next/Previous Question), and a modal question grid sheet. Outer canvas margins reduce to 16px (`1rem`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined layered surface strategy combining crisp 1px borders with ambient, neutral-tinted shadows to prevent interface fatigue during extended mock test sessions.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Flat `#F8FAFC` base without drop shadows.
- **Level 1 (Standard Card & Container Surfaces):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a crisp 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) and subtle ambient dispersion: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements & Hover States):** Exam question options on hover/selection, metric overview tiles: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Headers & Focus Rails):** Exam top timer header bar and navigation bars use a subtle white translucent backing with light boundary separation (`border-b border-slate-200`).
- **Level 4 (Modals, Confirmation Sheets, Dialogs):** Centered confirmation dialogs and critical submission modals use `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)` layered above a 40% opacity slate scrim (`#0F172A66`).

## Shapes

The design system adopts a balanced **Rounded** shape language (`roundedness: 2` with standard radii of `0.5rem` / `8px` up to `1rem` / `16px` for structural cards), establishing a clean, modern SaaS feel that remains structured and reliable.

- **Exam Cards & Panels:** Standardized at `0.75rem` (12px) to `1rem` (16px) corners to frame information cleanly.
- **Interactive Inputs & Multiple Choice Rows:** Styled at `0.5rem` (8px) for balanced click/tap targets.
- **Badges & Question Palette Dots:** Small square rounded pills (`6px` to `8px`) for palette matrices, with fully rounded (`9999px`) pills reserved exclusively for quick status indicators (e.g., active streaks, question counts).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#1E60F3` with pure white text, 8px corner radius, font-weight 600. On hover: shifts to `#1D4ED8` with a subtle elevation shift. Focus states display an outer 3px ring of `#93C5FD`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** White background with 1px `#CBD5E1` border and `#0F172A` text. On hover: background `#F8FAFC` and border `#94A3B8`.
- **Destructive / End Exam:** High-contrast solid dark navy (`#0F172A`) or soft red outline (`#EF4444`) to enforce deliberate, intentional actions.

### Multiple Choice Options (Exam Core Component)
- **Default State:** White background, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, 8px padding, 8px radius. Features an alphabetical index circle (A, B, C, D) on the left side with a neutral `#F1F5F9` background and `#475569` text.
- **Selected State:** Border transitions to 2px solid `#1E60F3` with a soft blue background wash (`#EFF6FF`). Index indicator turns solid `#1E60F3` with bold white text.
- **Hover State:** Border transitions to `#94A3B8` on non-selected items.

### Question Palette Grid
- Matrix of compact, square-rounded numeric items (`36px × 36px` on desktop, `32px × 32px` on mobile).
- **Answered:** Solid `#10B981` background, white text.
- **Not Answered / Skipped:** Crisp `#F8FAFC` background with 1px `#CBD5E1` border and dark text.
- **Marked for Review:** Solid `#8B5CF6` background, white text.
- **Current Active Question:** Outer high-contrast 2px ring in `#1E60F3`.

### Cards & Dashboards
- **Exam / Series Cards:** Pure white surface, 12px corner radius, 1px `#E2E8F0` border. Houses topic icon inside a soft pastel rounded tile (e.g., blue, green, violet, or amber tinted background), followed by title, test count, and full-width or aligned action trigger.
- **Metric Cards:** Crisp layout grouping an icon tile, numerical score (e.g., `78%`, `72 / 160`), percentile status pill, and secondary breakdown labels.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- Crisp `#FFFFFF` backgrounds bordered with 1px `#CBD5E1`. Default text is `#0F172A` with `#94A3B8` placeholder coloring.
- Focus displays an active `#1E60F3` border with a 3px matching ring blur (`rgba(30, 96, 243, 0.15)`).
