PM Gati Shakti Infrastructure Milestone & Multimodal Logistics Parks

target Target: SSC CGL, UPSC Prelims, Indian Railways (RRB)

Banking & Economy timer 2 min read

RBI Monetary Policy Committee Keeps Repo Rate Unchanged at 6.50%

target Target: IBPS PO, SBI PO, RBI Grade B, SEBI Grade A
  • Stance remains focused on 'Withdrawal of Accommodation' with a 5:1 majority to align inflation with the 4% target.
  • Real GDP growth projection for FY25 retained at 7.2% backed by robust manufacturing and rural recovery.
  • Standing Deposit Facility (SDF) rate stays at 6.25%, and Marginal Standing Facility (MSF) at 6.75%.
lightbulb Why It Matters for Exams

Core banking exam questions: Section 45ZB of RBI Act 1934, Composition of MPC (3 internal + 3 external members), difference between Repo vs SDF.

Science & Defense timer 2 min read

ISRO Successfully Conducts Hot Test for Next-Gen Launch Vehicle (NGLV) Cryogenic Engine

target Target: UPSC Prelims (GS-3), SSC CHSL, State PCS
  • Conducted at ISRO Propulsion Complex (IPRC) in Mahendragiri, Tamil Nadu for 720 seconds in high-altitude simulation.
  • NGLV (codenamed 'Soorya') designed to carry 30 tonnes to Low Earth Orbit (LEO) with reusable booster architecture.
  • Propellant combination utilizes LOX-Methane (Methalox) offering higher specific impulse and clean combustion.
lightbulb Why It Matters for Exams

High probability question: Comparison of Methalox vs Hydrolox fuel, payloads planned for Bharatiya Antariksh Station (BAS) 2035 mission.

International Summits timer 2 min read

16th BRICS Summit in Kazan: Key Declarations on Financial Messaging System

target Target: UPSC GS-2, Banking Awareness, CDS / AFCAT
  • Adoption of the 'Kazan Declaration' emphasizing reform of the Bretton Woods Institutions and IMF quota formula.
  • Introduction of 'BRICS Pay' initiative and Cross-Border Payment System (BCBPS) to settle trade in local national currencies.
  • First full summit including expanded members: Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran, and UAE alongside founding nations.
lightbulb Why It Matters for Exams

Common exam focus: BRICS New Development Bank (NDB) headquarters (Shanghai), Contingent Reserve Arrangement (CRA), member accession chronology.
