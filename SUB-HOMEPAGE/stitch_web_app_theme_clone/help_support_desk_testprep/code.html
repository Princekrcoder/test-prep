Frequently Asked Support Questions

Quick, battle-tested solutions to the most common exam interruptions and account situations.

Do not panic. Our CBT engine is built with client-side local caching and continuous auto-sync. If your internet connection drops or your browser window crashes:

  1. Re-open the same browser or app and navigate to Mock Tests > Ongoing Sessions.
  2. Click Resume Test. Your remaining timer countdown and marked responses are securely loaded from the snapshot cache.
  3. You have up to 15 minutes of buffer to reconnect before the system marks the session as automatically submitted.

Note: Test timer does not penalize you for minor latency blips under 60 seconds.

You can challenge any answer key within 48 hours of test completion directly from your analytics page:

  1. Open your test scorecard and click into the specific question solution view.
  2. Click the small flag icon “Report Discrepancy / Challenge Answer” in the top-right corner of the stem.
  3. Submit your textbook citation (NCERT, standard engineering reference, or official gazette) and a short proof note.

Our subject matter committee audits all challenges twice weekly. If an error is upheld, all candidate scorecards and percentile ranks for that mock test are universally recalibrated with bonus marks.

If money was debited from your bank account or UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) but your subscription pass was not immediately activated:

  • Automated UPI Reversals: Usually settle back to your source account within 2 to 4 hours per NPCI guidelines.
  • Credit / Debit Cards & Net Banking: Depend on banking gateway clearing protocols and take 3 to 5 business days.

You can track or expedite this by providing the 12-digit UTR/RRN number in the support ticket form above.
