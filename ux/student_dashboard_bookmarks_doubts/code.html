functions Topic: Bayesian Probability & Manufacturing Quality Control

Factory Line A manufactures 60% of electronic controllers with a defect rate of 2%. Factory Line B manufactures the remaining 40% with a defect rate of 5%. If a randomly tested controller from the assembled inventory is defective, what is the exact posterior probability that it was produced on Line B?

P(B|D) = [ P(D|B) · P(B) ] / [ P(D|A)·P(A) + P(D|B)·P(B) ] Bayes Formulation
A 0.375 (3 / 8)
B 0.500 (1 / 2)
Your Pick
C 0.625 (5 / 8 = 62.5%)
Correct Key
D 0.714 (5 / 7)
lightbulb Fast Integer Shortcut (TCS Strategy): Saves 90 seconds

Assume total production of 1,000 controllers.
• Line A = 600 units → 2% defects = 12 defective units.
• Line B = 400 units → 5% defects = 20 defective units.
• Total defects = 12 + 20 = 32 units. Posterior Line B = 20 / 32 = 5 / 8 = 0.625.

A compound stepped pulley consists of two concentric cylinders with moment of inertia I = 0.48 kg·m². The inner hub has radius r₁ = 0.15 m carrying weight m₁ = 4 kg, while the outer rim with radius r₂ = 0.35 m carries weight m₂ = 2 kg on opposing ropes. Dynamic Relation: Στ = I·α → α = (m₂·r₂ - m₁·r₁)·g / [ I + m₁·r₁² + m₂·r₂² ]
